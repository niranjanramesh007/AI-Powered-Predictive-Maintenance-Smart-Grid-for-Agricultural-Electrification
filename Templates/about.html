About the Project

The Smart Farm Energy Assistant is an AI-powered tool designed to help farmers optimize their electricity usage and water consumption. By analyzing equipment data and crop requirements, we provide actionable insights to save costs and protect equipment.

🚀 How It Works

1. Data Input: Farmers select their specific equipment (Pumps, Motors) and the crop they are cultivating.

2. AI Analysis: Our system calculates the exact water requirement for the land size and season, then converts that into optimal pump run-time.

3. Predictive Maintenance: The AI predicts the remaining lifespan of equipment and checks if the local transformer is at risk of overload.
